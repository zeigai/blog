Trading more often can feel like progress. Every new position looks like another opportunity, and sitting on the sidelines can feel like missing out. 

The evidence suggests otherwise. Frequent trading can increase costs, expose traders to more decisions under pressure and, in some cases, reflect overconfidence rather than a clear trading edge. More  traders can mean more activity without better results.
